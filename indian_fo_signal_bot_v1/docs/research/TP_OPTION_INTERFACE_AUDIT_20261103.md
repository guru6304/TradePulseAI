## Authority and ownership

The combined Agent 2 prompt and final Stage 1 specification section 7 define `TP-OPTION-FIT-1.0` as a non-directional contract-fitness and risk overlay on a separately qualified underlying thesis. It may choose an exact buy-to-open CE for bullish or PE for bearish research; OI, Greeks, IV and displayed depth are optional context. They neither identify institutional inventory nor create a new directional alpha rule.

The authoritative [strategy integration contract](../agent-contracts/TP_STRATEGY_INTEGRATION_CONTRACT_V1.md), [UI/API contract](../agent-contracts/TP_UI_API_CONTRACT_V1.md) and [master status](../project/TRADEPULSE_MASTER_STATUS.md) preserve Main's ownership of Option Apex detection/forward admission, scanner, ranking, account safety, portfolio and persistence. ## Option observation capture and presence semantics

[option_observations.py](../../option_observations.py) is transport-free **inspection**, not an executable quote contract. Its parser supports three distinct endpoint declarations:

| Endpoint constant | Raw fields mapped | Required unavailable distinction |
|---|---|---|
| `UPSTOX_V2_CHAIN` (`upstox.v2.option_chain`) | Exact leg token/underlying/expiry/strike/side; market LTP, volume, OI, raw `prev_oi`, bid/ask price/quantity; optional delta/gamma/theta/vega/IV/pop | Raw `prev_oi` has no independently dated prior-close semantics. Source timestamps and unit normalization remain unavailable. |
| `UPSTOX_V3_GREEKS` (`upstox.v3.option_greeks`) | Nested exact `instrument_token`; `last_price`, ltq, volume, OI, IV and Greeks | `cp` is mapped to **previous_close price**, never previous OI. Bid/ask and previous OI remain ABSENT. Missing requested tokens are listed rather than manufactured. |
| `ANGEL_GREEKS` (`angelone.v1.option_greeks`) | Exact underlying/expiry/strike/side resolved through retained dated declaration; delta/gamma/theta/vega, impliedVolatility and tradeVolume | No quote, OI, LTP or source-clock authority is inferred from the Greeks response. |

`_fields` at line 210 preserves each of 17 possible fields as `{presence, raw_value, numeric_value, raw_path, normalized_value, unit_status}`. `presence` is exactly `ABSENT`, `NULL` or `OBSERVED`; observed numeric zero remains zero. Missing/null does not become zero. Counts reject fractional/negative/bool values; signed Greeks remain signed raw numbers. `normalized_value` stays null and `unit_status` is `UNAVAILABLE_UNIT_CONTRACT`.

`parse_option_capture` at 230 checks exact request/master scope, retained master integrity, explicitly offset timestamps and `received_at <= available_at <= decision_at`. It retains original raw text/hash and source locators. It emits `tradepulse.option-capture.v1`, `status:STRUCTURALLY_VALID_UNQUALIFIED`, source_time null / `UNAVAILABLE_SOURCE_TIME`, and false `eligible`, `actionable`, `historical_replay_allowed` on every observation and envelope. Capabilities explicitly say source time, normalized units, dated prior-close OI and executable depth are unavailable; publisher authority and replay dataset are not established. Local capture/availability times remain caller declarations, not proof of the exchange clock.

Raw magnitudes differ by endpoint even in the existing fixtures (e.g. IV 12.5 versus 0.125). The parser intentionally does not guess an IV scale, theta calendar/trading-day basis, vega per-point/per-decimal unit, gamma unit, quantity scaling or source freshness. An optional research Greek scenario must require a distinct explicit unit/time-basis declaration and label the approximation; it must not convert these raw fields to an actual execution price.

## OI and displayed depth

`compare_oi` at `option_observations.py:437` accepts `tradepulse.oi-comparison.v1` inputs with full option identity, value, quantity unit, observed/available clocks, session date, source reference, provenance and baseline kind. It joins the **entire identity including source revision**, requires matching units, ordered clocks and current freshness. INTRADAY requires same session and declared alignment bound. PRIOR_CLOSE requires explicit preceding-session date and exact 15:30 IST observation; that declaration is not a verified exchange calendar. UNKNOWN baseline semantics remain unavailable.

Only `SYNTHETIC_FIXTURE` observations with known same units and supported baseline semantics can produce delta arithmetic. Unknown units produce `UNAVAILABLE_UNIT_CONTRACT`; unverified captures produce `UNQUALIFIED_SOURCE`; unknown baseline produces `UNAVAILABLE_BASELINE_SEMANTICS`. `prior_close_authority` remains `NOT_ESTABLISHED`, with eligibility/actionability/replay false. Do not compute a genuine prior-close OI change from raw `prev_oi` or subtract mismatched units/contracts.

`inspect_depth` at line 359 validates 1–30 sorted unique displayed levels with positive prices and integer nonnegative quantities and rejects crossed books. It computes coverage only for explicitly declared synthetic UNDERLYING_UNITS and requested size. Other source/unit combinations leave coverage null. Every result has `executable_depth:false`, eligibility/actionability false. Displayed quantity imbalance is not aggressor executed volume, event OFI, dealer inventory or institutional attribution. Summing multiple levels does not prove execution at the top price.

The capture/depth inspector permits a structurally locked book where bid equals ask; final Stage 1 option research policy calls for excluding locked/crossed/stale feed. The isolated version must state and test that stricter policy itself instead of treating inspection acceptance as execution acceptance.

## Existing historical Option Apex execution boundary

[option_execution.py](../../option_execution.py) is a separate existing historical/synthetic engine for `strat_3_option_apex_oi`, **not** a generic new fitness module. `_prepare` at 65 accepts `tradepulse.option-request.v1` with `master_json`, `dataset_json` and optional master/dataset manifests. Dataset `tradepulse.option-observations.v1` contains provider/mode, NIFTY or BANKNIFTY symbol, `premiums`, `underlying_candles` and `chains`. It supports OPTIDX only.

Its master is the archive schema from [instrument_archive.py](../../instrument_archive.py), not `ContractIdentity`: archive records use underlying `symbol` plus separate exact option `identifier`, expiry/strike/side/lot and explicit `tick_size`. Do not cast between these shapes by matching field names. Premium rows carry exact identifier/contract digest, five-minute start/availability clocks, bid/ask OHLC, observed opening `bid_size_units`/`ask_size_units`, depth time and volume units. It checks tick/grid/identity/geometry, linked chain OI baseline and chronological complete data.

`gate` at 210 and `run` at 224 enforce synthetic scope or independently trusted HISTORICAL_RESEARCH. LIVE-shaped historical inputs require archive/source manifests and existing publisher trust roots; those gates confer no current-live authority. Execution is a later option opening ask plus adverse tick-rounded impact, full-premium integer-lot capital/risk and observed opening size. Exit uses observed opening bid with conservative impact; unknown intrabar stop liquidity and missing exits retain unresolved position/reservation instead of inventing liquidation. Fees, signal geometry and all-or-none policies remain Main-owned and differ from the new 35%/1.8R research overlay. No new component should call this runner or change its Option Apex rules.

### Later Main integration dependency

The full final Stage 1 overlay remains a later integration objective. It will need a separately qualified underlying thesis and source/closed candle; authenticated dated listed contract plus premium tick; accepted session/calendar and stock membership; timestamped same-contract bid/ask/size with independently established units/freshness capability; real account/cost/source acceptance; and optional context. Defaults remain OFF. The first snapshot component cannot supply those operational guarantees. Unverified real-shaped input remains unavailable for executable acceptance and source-validity claims.

Proposed result handoff should retain `candidate_version`, schema/config identity, underlying thesis/candle/source, exact provider/token/expiry/strike/side/lot/tick/revision, as-of/observed/received/available times, reason/unavailable codes, field-presence/unit coverage, cost-model version, future revalidation policy, size/risk, and `confidence_type:ordinal_quality_not_probability`. Selection and actual assumed future fill are separate receipts. A future trigger must revalidate the identical contract and current quote; absent quote yields `NO_FILL` / `QUOTE_UNAVAILABLE`. Ask-plus-impact entry and later bid-minus-impact exit must use adverse Decimal tick rounding with spread embedded once.

The independent premium stop is `floor_tick((1-loss_fraction)*entry)` with loss fractions 0.25/0.35/0.45 and default 0.35; target is `ceil_tick(entry+1.8*(entry-stop))`. Underlying invalidation is a separate observed exit condition; current Greeks never manufacture the option bid. Final rules permit new entry through 14:45 and require strategy exit by 15:15; expiry-day entries are excluded. Nearest two eligible actually-listed non-expiry-day expiries are selected before fitness ranking. Pilot three-second age and index 1% / stock 2% spread bands are unvalidated comparisons and require explicit cadence/field support.

No shared schema migration is needed for offline isolation. Main review is required before adding real adapters, source authority, new scanner IDs/flags, API persistence, underlying/quote trigger linkage, account duplicate/capital coordination or any prospective capture. Existing source gates and barriers must not be relaxed to make research fixtures pass.
