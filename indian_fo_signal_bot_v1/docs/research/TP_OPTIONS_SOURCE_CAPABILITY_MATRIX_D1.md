## Classification and method

Each classification applies to the specific field, surface and claim, not the whole provider. **DOCUMENTED** = explicit official schema/statement; **CONDITIONAL** = offered subject to account, plan, coverage or agreement; **UNVERIFIED** = meaning, operational behavior or rights not established by inspected public evidence; **UNAVAILABLE** = absent from the cited response/product, or explicitly unsupported; **CONTRADICTORY** = inspected official statements disagree or an example and schema cannot be reconciled safely. UNAVAILABLE is not a claim that no other product could supply the field.

These are documentation classifications. They must not be renamed the existing package's `VERIFIED` state. That state means supplied-input checks only. D1 establishes no `source_qualified`, `prospective_ready`, `forward_entry_allowed` or historical execution permission.

All linked sources below were consulted on the assessment date. **Undated** means the source showed no reliable page publication/revision date; copyright, crawler dates, SDK HEAD and example timestamps are not revisions. Official support/forum statements are identified separately from specifications. Direct SmartAPI pages rejected full browser opens; their indexed official sections and the official SDK/forum were usable, so Angel conclusions have that access limitation. Current Dhan guide navigation sometimes rendered the wrong document; versioned v2 references and named official support articles were used instead. No missing meaning was repaired from community guesses.

## Calendar and documentation conflicts requiring a dated fixture

The [NSE timings page](https://www.nseindia.com/static/market-data/market-timings) currently lists equity derivatives closing15:40, while its footer says updated2025-08-04. The announcement and [NSE CAS page](https://www.nseindia.com/market-data/closing-auction-session) also describe different auction window details. D1 does not change approved algorithms or their research14:45/15:15 cutoffs.
