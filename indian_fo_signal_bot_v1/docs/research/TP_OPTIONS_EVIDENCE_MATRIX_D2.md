## Separate documentation from supplied-data checks

`DocState` is `DOCUMENTED | CONDITIONAL | UNVERIFIED | UNAVAILABLE | CONTRADICTORY`. It classifies one claim about a named source surface/version. A documented field name does not prove its value, quantitative unit, event time, delivery or permitted use in a supplied artifact.

`DataState` is `VERIFIED | UNAVAILABLE | STALE | INVALID | NOT_ESTABLISHED`. Existing `option_intelligence` uses VERIFIED only for checks on supplied inputs. It never establishes publisher authenticity, listing authority, account entitlement or execution permission. A D1 DOCUMENTED statement does not automatically map to any DataState; the actual observation, supporting evidence and applicable inspector decide the scoped result.

Preserve the reason for every unresolved item. Missing observations remain missing; undefined clock/unit meaning remains unestablished; malformed or conflicting terms remain invalid; genuinely old observations remain stale. A locally authored revision, hash or `ACTIVE` declaration cannot repair an authority gap. Optional evidence is assessed independently and cannot compensate for a missing mandatory key.

## Audit record and completion limits

For each reviewed claim, retain: stable evidence key; provider and surface/version; field/path and raw unit; source URL or actual artifact locator/hash; immutable local capture revision; vendor revision only if supplied; original publication/effective dates when known; checked-at and available-at clocks; DocState and scope; corresponding supplied DataState/reasons when evaluated; unresolved question; reviewed answer/artifact reference; and coverage/uncertainty. Missing publication dates stay null; response examples and crawler dates are not revisions. Preserve conflicts and prior versions instead of editing them into agreement.

A later source review must inspect actual permitted artifacts against these questions; a documentary answer alone cannot prove operational latency, packet completeness or market delivery. Any bounded capture still requires separate authorization and independently evidenced rights. Endpoint-specific throttle/subscription limits must be respected together; they are not freshness SLAs.
